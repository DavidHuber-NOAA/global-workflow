###################################
Tier 1: Production-only jobs (GEFS)
###################################

Jobs available in the GEFS system at this tier. A job may not appear in every configuration.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 75

   * - Job
     - Purpose
   * - ``postsnd``
     - Produce forecast model soundings at select locations
   * - ``awips_20km_1p0deg``, ``awips``
     - Regrid forecasts to 20-km and 1-deg AWIPS grids
   * - ``gempak``
     - Converts atmosphere forecast output to gempak format
